Recognising Excellence: NBL Awards and Performance

The NBL has a history of recognising outstanding players, coaches and teams through its annual awards. This visualisation explores these achievements, looking at what makes a player valuable, the importance of coaching and defence, and the connection between individual success and championship glory. It also highlights the Australian-born players who have helped shape the league over the years.

01 — What determines an MVP?

MVP vs Scoring Leader

Over the years, the NBL’s top scorer has often gone hand in hand with winning MVP. In many seasons, the player putting up the most points was also recognised as the league’s most valuable player. However, there are seasons where a different player takes home the MVP award, showing that being the best player is about more than just scoring points.

Games Played vs Minutes

Winning MVP often comes down to more than talent; staying healthy and available is just as important. A full season on the court gives players more opportunities to make a consistent impact and prove their value.

02 — Coaching Excellence

NBL Coach of the Year — Team Wins

Coach of the Year winners typically guide their teams to around 20 wins per season, winning approximately 66% of their games. This highlights the importance of building a winning team and maintaining consistency throughout the season.

Coach of the Year Championship Results

The pie chart shows that only around 30% of Coach of the Year winners went on to win the NBL Championship. This highlights that a successful regular season doesn't always guarantee a title. With the playoffs played over a series of games, opposing teams have time to adjust their tactics, making the championship a different challenge altogether.

03 — Defensive Performance

Compare defensive statistics and explore the characteristics of NBL Defensive Player of the Year winners.

NBL Defensive Player of the Year: Steals vs Blocks vs Height

The bubble chart shows that Defensive Player of the Year winners make their impact in different ways. Some stand out through steals, disrupting opposition attacks, while others use their height and strength to protect the basket with blocks. It highlights that great defence isn't just about being the tallest player, but about using different skills to make life difficult for opponents

NBL Defensive Player of the Year by Position

Interestingly, point guards have won the Defensive Player of the Year award more than any other position. This is somewhat unexpected, as centres and forwards are often associated with defensive roles such as blocking shots and protecting the paint.

Defensive Winners and Championships

Having the best defender does not guarantee a championship. Scoring and overall team performance appear more influential in winning titles.
